About Us

This website was created as part of the Rollins College Computer Science Capstone course for Rollins' Collaborative Research Fellowship. Every aspect of this service has been developed over the course of the past 4 months through the hard work of Maggie Kuffskie, Mara Schimmel, and Olivia Christie.

Maggie Kuffskie

Maggie Kuffskie is a physics and computer science double major interested in computational astrophysics. She is planning to attend graduate school at the University of Alabama in the fall for a PhD in physics.

Mara Schimmel

Mara Schimmel is a computer science senior at Rollins College. She also is finishing a minor in both math and Jewish studies.

Olivia Christie

Olivia Christie is a computer science major and classical studies minor at Rollins College. She enjoys listening to synthpop and playing games on her laptop in her freetime.
