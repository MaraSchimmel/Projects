Can I do research outside my major department?

You can complete research in any department, provided you find a faculty member who is able to be your research advisor. Having some knowledge of a field will make writing a proposal and conducting research easier, but you do not need to be an expert in what you want to research. Many projects come as the result of a student wanting to explore inter-departmental interests!

When does research happen?

CRF research takes place during the summer, over the course of 6-8 weeks. The exact dates of a research project will vary based on student and faculty availability, but most projects are completed between late May and early July.

How do I apply to the Collaborative Research Fellowship?

To apply to the CRF program, you must write a research proposal. The length of this proposal will vary depending on the department's standards, but typical proposals range from 3 to 8 pages. Proposals must include a literature review of existing research, a summary of the research that will be completed, and an explanation of how the proposed research will benefit the overall community. Proposals are written with the help of the research advisor, but are meant to be written primarily by the student.

Additionally, students and faculty will have to submit a cover letter explaining their qualifications and any other relevant information. All information will be submitted on the CRF portal.

Can I do research as part of a team?

While many students' research builds towards a larger goal, each student must create an individual application to the CRF program. Group projects are expected to be of a greater scope than individual projects to ensure that each student receives adequate experience. Additionally, students who work as a group will still have to complete individual final write-ups, where they explain the results of the completed research.

Is the research paid?

CRF research is a paid opportunity. The exact amount varies year by year and is subject to Rollins available funding. Details on exact funding will be released at a later date.

Is housing provided during research?

Students who are accepted to the CRF program will be asked if they need housing during the research period. If you do, on-campus housing will be provided in one of the Rollins College dorms. Students in previous years have been housed in Lakeside or Sutton depending on space availability.

My account is locked, who do I contact for help?

If your account is locked, it is likely due to either too many incorrect password attempts or an access issue. Please contact the CRF administrators at crfrollins@gmail.com.

When is the application deadline?

The exact application deadline varies year to year, but typically falls around late February to early March. Proposals will typically start being written in late November, but this date may vary depending on your research advisor. The exact deadlines will be released soon.

Who can do CRF research?

CRF research is available to undergraduate students in both CLA and Holt programs. Students must attend Rollins College as an undergraduate in the semesters preceding and following the summer that research would be conducted. This means that the earliest research could be conducted in the year following a student's freshmen year, and the latest research could be conducted in the year preceding their senior year.
